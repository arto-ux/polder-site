POLDER

Wall it off. Watch it fall. A free territory-capture arcade game for iPhone.

How to play

Swipe to steer the red cube. Leave the outer ring to cut across the slab, then return to solid ground to close the cut: everything no enemy can reach breaks away and falls. Claim the shown share of the slab to clear the level. If an enemy touches your open cut, you lose a life.

Free, with nothing attached

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Your high score is stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.
