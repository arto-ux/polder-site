Privacy Policy

Polder for iPhone · Effective 10 November 2026

Summary

Polder does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network features.

What stays on your device

The game saves your high score in the app's local storage on your iPhone so it can show it next time. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

Polder contains no third-party code, SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Children

Because Polder collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
